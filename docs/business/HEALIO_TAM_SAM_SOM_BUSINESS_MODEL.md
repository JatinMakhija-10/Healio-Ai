### Pillar 1: Healio AYUSH & Medicine E-Commerce Marketplace

#### 1. Concept & Contextual Commerce
Unlike standalone e-pharmacies where users search manually for drugs, Healio.AI utilizes a **Diagnosis-Driven Contextual Commerce Engine**. When the AI Triage detects a specific health imbalance (e.g., *Pitta-Hyperacidity*, *Vata-Joint Stiffness*, or *Metabolic Sluggishness*), the diagnosis output card dynamically displays curated, AYUSH-certified remedies (*Gulkand, Dashmool, Trikatu, Ashwagandha*) and OTC health products.

#### 2. Phased Monetization Roadmap
* **Phase 1: Affiliate Referral Model (Months 1–3)**
  * Integrate affiliate APIs with established brands (Kapiva, Zandu, 1mg, Baidyanath, Kerala Ayurveda).
  * **Revenue Model:** **10% – 20% affiliate commission** per successful conversion.
  * **Advantage:** Zero inventory liability, immediate monetization from Day 1.
* **Phase 2: Direct Multi-Vendor Marketplace (Months 4–18)**
  * Verified Ayurvedic and herbal D2C merchants list products directly on Healio.AI.
  * Automated payment splitting via **Razorpay Route**: 80% disbursed to merchant, **20%–25% platform commission** retained by Healio.AI.
* **Phase 3: Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) Private Label (Year 2+)**
  * Launch "Healio Certified" co-branded or private label herbal formulations for top-selling high-frequency conditions (e.g., Sleep & Stress, Digestive Health, Immunity).
  * **Revenue Model:** **60%+ Gross Margins**.

---

### Pillar 2: Doctor & Specialist Consultation Marketplace

#### 1. Specialist Onboarding & Verification
Healio.AI connects patients to verified medical specialists across three streams:
* **Allopathy (MBBS / MD):** General Physician, Internal Medicine, Dermatology, Pediatrics.
* **Ayurveda (BAMS / MD Ayurveda):** Kaya Chikitsa, Panchakarma, Nadi Pariksha specialists.
* **Homeopathy (BHMS / MD Homeopathy):** Chronic condition management.

#### 2. Consultation Commission Architecture
* **Pricing Range:** ₹300 to ₹1,200 per consultation slot (set by the doctor or standardized by platform tier).
* **Marketplace Commission:** **20% to 25% flat take-rate** on every completed appointment.
* **Payment Split Escrow (Razorpay Route):**
  * Patient pays ₹500 via UPI/Cards.
  * Razorpay Route automatically directs **₹400 (80%)** to the doctor's bank account and **₹100 (20%)** to Healio.AI’s operating account upon session completion.
* **No-Show & Cancellation Protection:**
  * Patients cancelling <2 hours before the slot forfeit 50% fee (Healio retains 20%, doctor receives 30% retention bonus).

#### 3. Sponsored Search & Doctor Promotion ("Featured Listings")
* Doctors can pay to boost their visibility in search results within their specialty.
* **Monetization:** Cost-Per-Click (CPC) of ₹15–₹30 per profile view OR a Flat Sponsored Listing fee of **₹1,500 – ₹3,000 / month per doctor**.
* **Ethical Guardrail:** Sponsored profiles are explicitly marked with a "Sponsored Specialist" tag to maintain patient trust and legal compliance.

## 7. INVESTOR DEFENSE & RISK MITIGATION PROTOCOLS

### 1. Regulatory & Medical Safety Risk
* **Risk:** SaMD (Software as a Medical Device) regulatory scrutiny or misdiagnosis liability.
* **Mitigation:**
  * Built-in sub-200ms **Red Flag Scan** overriding AI for high-risk symptoms (chest pain, stroke, severe shortness of breath).
  * Prominent disclaimers adhering to **Telemedicine Practice Guidelines 2020**. Healio.AI explicitly acts as an informational decision-support tool, not a replacing physician.
  * Automated filing under CDSCO Class A SaMD framework.

### 2. Patient Data Privacy Risk
* **Risk:** Non-compliance with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023.
* **Mitigation:**
  * Supabase PostgreSQL database configured with strict **Row-Level Security (RLS)** on all patient tables.
  * LocalStorage option for guest sessions (zero server footprint until explicit user consent).
