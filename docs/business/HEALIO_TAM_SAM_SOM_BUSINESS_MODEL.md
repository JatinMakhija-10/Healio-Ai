### 2.3 Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) — Corrected & Audited Math

SAM filters the TAM down to India's **221 Million digitally active smartphone health-seekers** (26% of 850M smartphone users) across Tier 1, Tier 2, and tech-enabled Tier 3 cities.

#### Itemized SAM Derivation:

1. **Online Doctor Consultations (Allopathy + AYUSH):**
   $$\text{Consultation SAM} = 221\text{M users} \times 2.2 \text{ consults/yr} \times ₹450 \text{ Fee} = \mathbf{₹21,879 \text{ Crore (\$2.32 Billion USD)}}$$

2. **AYUSH Remedies & Herbal OTC E-Commerce:**
   $$\text{AYUSH E-Commerce SAM} = 221\text{M users} \times ₹2,400 \text{ annual spend} = \mathbf{₹53,040 \text{ Crore (\$5.61 Billion USD)}}$$

3. **B2C Consumer Health Subscriptions:**
   $$\text{B2C Subscriptions SAM} = 221\text{M users} \times 4\% \text{ adoption} \times ₹1,499/\text{yr} = \mathbf{₹1,325 \text{ Crore (\$140.2 Million USD)}}$$

4. **B2B Doctor SaaS (Arovia Pro Workspace) — Corrected Math:**
   $$\text{Total Addressable Doctors} = 600,000 \text{ Practitioners} \times 25\% \text{ tech adoption} = 150,000 \text{ Doctors}$$
   $$\text{B2B SaaS SAM} = 150,000 \text{ Doctors} \times ₹23,988/\text{year} = \mathbf{₹359.8 \text{ Crore (\$38.1 Million USD)}}$$
   *(Note: Corrected from erroneous ₹3,598 Cr decimal slip in previous un-audited draft).*

#### TOTAL AUDITED SAM:
$$\mathbf{\text{Total SAM}} = ₹21,879\text{ Cr} + ₹53,040\text{ Cr} + ₹1,325\text{ Cr} + ₹359.8\text{ Cr} = \mathbf{₹76,603.8 \text{ Crore (~₹76,604 Cr)}}$$
$$\mathbf{\text{Total SAM in USD (@ ₹94.5/\$)}} = \frac{₹76,604 \text{ Crore}}{94.5} = \mathbf{\$8.11 \text{ Billion USD}}$$
